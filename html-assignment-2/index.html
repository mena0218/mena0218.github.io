Good News everyone who reads this: I am not a robot! Thank God, am I right?! In all seriousness, I am a humain being who's interests mainly lie in video games, F1, the internet and watching movies and other content creators. I also have other micro interests in comedy, therapy related topics, listening to the many soundtracks of video games that I have experienced and video editing which is something I'd like to do in the future. Speaking of something I'd like to do in the future, I'd love to try my hand at voice acting since I see a lot of people who do it as a job have so much fun doing it. It seems like a fun endeavor and gig job to do that would be a fun challenge. I can already imitate a British/Australian accent, so frankly I already have it in me right?

True courage is about knowing not when to take a life, but when to spare one. -Gandalf the Grey

I am mostly an introvert in everyday life. You can catch me being in a more extroverted tone especially when I am in a state of pure happiness and euphoria when having an engaging conversation with someone like family, friends or a stranger that I've resonated with. However, I am mostly quiet mainly because I like to listen more than I like to speak. I am comforted by sound as it makes everything more authentic, transparent and all together more real than sight. One of my favourite sounds is the sound of an F1 engine. I can't imagine my life without the influence of the sport since it came during my very earliest years in this world. Hearing it is almost like a portal into those pure moments where I've truly felt the happiest of all. I especially adore the V8 and the late versions of the 1.6 Litre V6 turbo hybrid engines. This year will be my 18th year of watching it since I first laid eyes on it back in 2008. What a year that was...
